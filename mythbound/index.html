PATCH NOTES

Update 1.4.2 — The companion situation

A small update with a surprisingly large number of sheep.

Nov 15, 2026

Fixed an issue where Herder companions could occupy chairs during party meetings. Resolved a rare issue causing Saints to face the wrong direction while casting a blessing. Improved the visibility of Breaker impact effects. Known issue: some players remain convinced that standing in hostile ground effects is a valid strategy. This is not a bug.
