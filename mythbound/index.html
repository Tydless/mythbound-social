Gameplay

✧

Discover a world of possibilities. Find your own way to adventure, share the journey with friends and choose the path that will shape your legend.

Exploration

From the familiar fields of home to lands of myth and wonder, discover the world at your own pace. Explore settlements, hidden landmarks, dungeons and the stories waiting beyond the next horizon.

Explore with Friends

Share your adventures with fellow travelers, explore together and take on dungeons and raids as a party.

Symbiosis System

A dedicated cooperative system for shared adventures.

System details forthcoming

Join or Found a Guild

Find a community of kindred adventurers or create a guild of your own. Build friendships, organize adventures and find companions for your next challenge.

Guild advantages

Shared adventures, a regular group of players and a place to belong.

Guild mechanics and specific benefits forthcoming

Class Changes

Begin with one of six base classes and discover the advanced Paths that can shape your class into something new. Each class offers its own specializations and a different way to express your playstyle.
